**Robotics Research Intern, ETF Robotics Laboratory, University of Belgrade (Nov 2025 – Jul 2026).**
[Challenge](https://www.intrinsic.ai/events/ai-for-industry-challenge) · [Code](https://github.com/etf-robotics/etf_robotics_aic)

Our lab's entry in the AI for Industry Challenge organized by Intrinsic and Open Robotics: teach a robot to insert cables into industrial connectors.

- Developed imitation- and reinforcement-learning policies for cable insertion in **NVIDIA Isaac Lab**.
- Built the simulation scenes in **OpenUSD**.
- Set up a containerized **ROS 2 (Kilted Kaiju) / Gazebo** environment to carry policies from simulation to the real robot.

Related: our ETRAN 2026 paper on bridging simulation and reality for robot assembly tasks (see [publications]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }})).
