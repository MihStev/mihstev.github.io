**ETF Robotics Laboratory, Aug – Sep 2026.** Franka Emika Panda (7-DoF), ROS 2, Gazebo.

I built a nonlinear model of the ball's flight and of the ball–paddle impact, and used it inside a **model-based trajectory-optimization controller** that steers the paddle toward stable, repeated bounces. The controller is validated in Gazebo, for transfer to the real Panda.
