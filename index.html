I'm interested in how robots can learn control from data rather than having it hand-tuned, through imitation and reinforcement learning, and world models that let them plan.

  • NowMSc MVA, ENS Paris-Saclay · French Government Scholar (BGF)
  • ResearchIL/RL policies for cable insertion in the Intrinsic AI for Industry Challenge, ETF Robotics Lab
  • ThesisACT vs. Diffusion Policy on 30k demos: lateral error is the main obstacle; more data cut it 3.5× (41.2 → 11.8 mm)
  • World modelsAction-conditioned video diffusion on BAIR robot data: the arm moves in the commanded direction 84.8% of the time
  • ControlModel-predictive control for intercepting a thrown ping-pong ball with a Franka Panda (in progress)
